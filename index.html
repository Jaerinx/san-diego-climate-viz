San Diego Climate Over the Last Century

Five static exploratory charts built with D3.js: NOAA CMIP6 historical simulations (GFDL-ESM4) for temperature, precipitation, and sea level near San Diego, plus U.S. Census county population density (1925–2014).

1. Warming trend in annual mean temperature

Year-to-year variability with a 5-year moving average reveals gradual warming near San Diego over the last nine decades of the historical run.

2. Temperature anomalies from a 1961–1990 baseline

Bars show departure from the late-20th-century climatological mean — a standard reference for detecting recent warming.

3. Modeled sea level near the San Diego coast

CMIP6 sea surface height (zos) at the nearest ocean grid cell shows long-term variability and rise relative to a 1961–1990 baseline (millimeters).

4. Year-to-year precipitation totals

Modeled annual rainfall shows Mediterranean-style wet winters and dry summers aggregated to yearly totals; dashed line marks the 1961–1990 mean.

5. San Diego County population density

U.S. Census decennial counts (red dots) define county population; density uses a fixed land area of 4,207 sq mi with linear interpolation between census years.

Data: NOAA Public CMIP6 on Google Cloud. Course context: DSC 106 Project 3. CMIP6 values are model simulations. Population data are from the U.S. Census, not CMIP6.
